# CITYWALK Brand System V1

Source of truth for the approved CITYWALK identity.

## Brand promise

**Explore more. Plan less.**

CITYWALK turns the time a traveler has into a walk they can actually follow.

## Official mark

The approved mark is the user-approved location pin above a flowing walking route. Do not substitute the previous multi-stop route mark or skyline illustration.

## Core colors

- **CITYWALK Navy** `#0E2C44` — location pin, wordmark, primary dark text.
- **CITYWALK Teal** `#019FA3` — walking route, motion, discovery accent.
- **CITYWALK Canvas** `#FAFAF8` — calm warm background for brand-led surfaces.
- **White** `#FFFFFF` — clean product surfaces.

Product UI may keep semantic colors for success, warning, danger, maps, and accessibility states. Brand colors should not replace semantic meaning.

## Wordmark

Write the product name as **CITYWALK** in uppercase. Keep the wordmark separate from the artwork so it stays crisp at small sizes and can be localized around without changing the mark.

## Logo usage

- Keep the pin and walking route together as one mark.
- Preserve the original proportions.
- Use transparent artwork on light or neutral backgrounds.
- Do not recolor the pin and route independently outside approved monochrome use.
- Do not stretch, rotate, add new shadows, or place other objects inside the clear space.
- In compact app chrome, show the mark with the CITYWALK wordmark when space permits.

## Product naming

- Visible product name: **CITYWALK**
- Domain: **getcitywalk.com**
- Preferred social handle: **@getcitywalk**
- CTA language: **Get CITYWALK** / **Build my walk** depending on intent.

## Creative system

Future generated images, videos, social cards, landing pages, and App Store creatives should derive from this Brand System. AI may compose around the logo but must not redraw, reinterpret, or mutate the approved mark.
